## What was built

### 1. The song in RAM — `live_bridge.c`

`live_bridge_map(const pattern_t *pats, song_t *sg)` is the one plain mapping function
(unit-tested in `tests/host/live_bridge_test.c`):

- **DRUMS A–G**: the first 7 patterns whose 909 `dpart` has a hit (8+ non-empty patterns cap at
  7). `dpart.hit[11][2]` and `dpart.accent[2]` are copied straight onto `drum_steps_t` (identical
  packing, both 32-step words), and `len` carries through (checked with a 64-step pattern).
- **BASS A–E**: the first 5 patterns with a 303 **A** line, as notes. Each `G_NOTE` step becomes
  a `note_t {step, note, vel, len}` with the part transpose applied, `vel` 127 accented / 88 not,
  `len` extended across a following run of `G_TIE` steps, and **`BS_SLIDE` carried through** as
  bit 7 of `vel` (X0X's rule: a note slides when the step before it sounded and was marked slide).
- **LEAD A–G**: the same for 303 **B**.
- **CHORDS and FX** stay empty.
- `key_clip[]` is the identity map; roles are filled with the model's engines and clip ranges.
- Motion lanes are marked `PARAM_NONE` (a zeroed lane would look like parameter 0 and fire every
  step).

The song and the `clip_engine_t` are `static` in `.pool`, rebuilt on every `live_bridge_enter`.

### 2. Audio — direct, sample-accurate, never through the main loop's queue

`engine_render` calls `live_bridge_render(n, &sink)` **instead of** `seq_advance` while LIVE is
active. The bridge runs `clip_engine_render` at `CLIP_ENGINE_FS = 44100` (asserted equal to
`sequencer.h`'s `FS` in `live_bridge.c`) and hands each clip event to `engine.c` in plain terms
(`live_ev_t`, no `model.h`). `engine.c`'s sink **renders the sub-block up to the event's sample
offset, then applies it to the voice directly** — `s_drum`, `s_bon`, `s_boff`, `apply_param`, the
same functions the sequencer's `SINK` uses:

| clip event | applied as |
|---|---|
| `LIVE_EV_DRUM` | `s_drum(0, 0, lane, accent ? 1.0 : accent_q7/127)` |
| `LIVE_EV_NOTE_ON` (BASS/LEAD) | `s_bon(0, part, note, vel&0x7F >= 100, vel>>7)` |
| `LIVE_EV_NOTE_OFF` | `s_boff(0, part)` |
| `LIVE_EV_MOTION` | `apply_param(target, voice, index, value)` |

**The ISR never calls `cq_put`.** `cq_put` now traps (`__builtin_trap`) on the host if it is
reached with `in_isr` set, which `engine_render` sets for its whole body (verified by planting a
`cq_put` in the sink: the host aborts with SIGILL). Each applied event is also passed to
`engine_tap` at its **exact sample**, so the runner's `events` golden shows non-multiple-of-256
timestamps (10 of the 12 `live_pads` events).

CHORDS/FX map to nothing yet (they have no clips until T5i).

### 3. Entering LIVE stops X0X synchronously; leaving releases cleanly

`engine_all_off()` (main loop) hands the ISR a "stop now + all off" flag that
`engine_render` handles at the start of the next block: it calls `bass303_note_off` on both 303s,
clears `seq.playing` and the transport requests, and clears each track's `sounding`/`held_note`.
So entering LIVE from a playing STUDIO cannot leave a 303 hung (`live_entry_stop` checks ten
entry phases). `live_bridge_exit` stops the clip transport and releases the 303s before handing
the audio back.

### 4. Modes — a long press on PLAY, on release

A **short press on PLAY acts on release** (released before the ~600 ms threshold); holding past
the threshold enters or leaves LIVE and **starts or stops neither transport**. The transport acts
once, on the release, in STUDIO and in LIVE. The hold shows a filling ring over the current
screen. The interaction model notes the release rule (`docs/product/interaction-model.md`, under
Modes).

### 5. The PADS layer (default)

- The 27 keys are clips in zone order; `song.key_clip` is the identity map.
- Tap a key: launch at the clip's quantize. Tap the playing clip: queue a stop. HOME + key:
  `clip_engine_launch_now`.
- **Key LEDs**: **off** empty, **dim** loaded/stopped, **lit** playing, **fast blink**
  (`ui.frame & 4`) queued to start, **slow blink** (`ui.frame & 32`) queued to stop. SCL is lit
  on the PLAY layer.
- The screen: `LIVE` header chip, layer badge, transport icon, BPM and `bar.beat`; a 5-row pad
  grid (2-letter role labels `DR BA LE CH FX` with clearance from the first cell, role colours,
  cell state, progress bar, focus arrow); footer `SCL play` / `hold PLAY exit`.

### 6. The PLAY layer

SCL toggles it. The keys play the focused role chromatically; the focused role is the last one
launched, or BASS at entry; ALGORITHM steps it. OCT−/OCT+ shift the octave. **A held note is
tracked per key**, so changing layer, focus or octave never retargets it: a key release always
releases the note that key started (`live_hold`). The header says `PLAY`; the screen shows the
role, its engine and the octave (fully inside the band).

### 7. Controls

- **SEL** toggles the layer (the physical SCL button). **Turning SELECT is inert in LIVE.**
- **K1–K4 are the LIVE performance knobs**, wired to real engine parameters and labelled per the
  design (`Main.dc.html`): K1 FILTER (`T_MST` `MST_DJF`), K2 SPACE (`FX_RV_LEVEL`), K3 ECHO
  (`FX_DL_LEVEL`), K4 DRIVE (`FX_DRIVE`). (Reverting unrecorded LIVE tweaks on exit is T10i.)
- ALGORITHM steps the focused role; the other buttons are inert in LIVE.

### 8. RAM

- `engine.c`: the delay line is 44,100 samples (1.0 s); `fxbus.c`'s `fx_retime` clamps
  `dl_time_ms` to `(dlen - 256)` samples. The LIVE `song_t` (14,352 B) and `clip_engine_t` (440 B)
  live in `.pool`.

| POOL used | 328,052 / 344,064 | 254,636 / 344,064 | **254,636 / 344,064** |

## Tests

**Unit** — `make unit-live` (`tests/run_tests.sh`, `make unit`, `make unit-core`) maps the project
and now also pins the `BS_SLIDE` carry, the 64-step second hit word, the 7-pattern cap, and the
bridge's real (non-stub) event stream (a capturing sink sees the DRUM lane and the BASS
note-on/off).

**Scenarios with goldens** (PNG at 1:1, dump JSON, events) — the four new ones each fail before
their fix and pass after:

| scenario | checks | fails on |
|---|---|---|
| `live_entry_stop` | enter LIVE from a playing STUDIO at 10 entry phases; the output is silent (`silence 50 -35`) within 50 ms every time | the hung 303 (-17.7 dBFS) |
| `live_press` | short PLAY acts on release in STUDIO and LIVE; a long press enters/leaves and toggles neither transport | acting on the press |
| `live_hold` | a key held across SCL and across ALGORITHM releases the note it started | the hang / wrong-part release |
| `live_autosave` | no flash write while LIVE is active; the autosave resumes after leaving | store_writes 6 during LIVE |
| `live_pads` | enter, launch, switch on the bar, HOME + key now, stop a role, leave | — |
| `live_play` | SCL, three notes, held note released on exit, X0X resumes | — |
| `live_leds` | off / dim / lit / fast blink / slow blink | — |

`golden.py` gained no changes; the runner gained `silence MS [DB]` and `keyblink K [slow]` (the
latter already used by `live_leds`). `expect mode/layer/focus/liveactive/liveclip/liveque/
livestop/liveused`, the LIVE-aware `bar/beat/step/wait-bar/playing`, and the `dump` `live` object
are unchanged from the first revision.

## Decisions / open

- **Leaving LIVE hands the transport back to X0X's sequencer, so clips do not carry on across the
  mode change.** The interaction model says they do; this is recorded in
  [open-questions.md](../docs/open-questions.md) (owner decision) until T6i/T7.
- The motion param encoding is pinned by T3 (`target << 12 | voice << 8 | index`, noted in
  `model.md`).
- K1–K4's revert-on-exit is T10i; the key→clip map stays the identity until LIVE SETUP (T10i).
- The bridge reconciles the model's and X0X's shared `song_t` name in its own translation unit;
  `live_bridge.h` speaks only plain types.
