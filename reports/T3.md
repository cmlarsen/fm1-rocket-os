which absorbs T4i: LIVE is built directly on the merged clip engine (`core/seq/clip_engine.*`).

## What was built

### 1. The song in RAM — `live_bridge.c`

`live_bridge_map(const pattern_t *pats, song_t *sg)` is the one plain mapping function
(unit-tested in `tests/host/live_bridge_test.c`):

- **DRUMS A–G**: the first 7 patterns whose 909 `dpart` has a hit. `dpart.hit[11][2]` and
  `dpart.accent[2]` are copied straight onto `drum_steps_t` (the packing is identical).
- **BASS A–E**: the first 5 patterns with a 303 **A** line, as notes. Each `G_NOTE` step becomes
  a `note_t {step, note, vel, len}` with the part transpose applied, `vel` 127 accented / 88 not,
  and `len` extended across a following run of `G_TIE` steps.
- **LEAD A–G**: the same for 303 **B**.
- **CHORDS and FX** stay empty.
- `key_clip[]` is the identity map; roles are filled with the model's engines
  (909 / 303 / 303 / FM6 / NOISE) and clip ranges.
- Motion lanes are marked `PARAM_NONE` (a zeroed lane would look like parameter 0 and fire every
  step — the first draft did exactly that).

The song and the `clip_engine_t` are `static` in `.pool` (`LIVE_POOL`), rebuilt on every
`live_bridge_enter`.

### 2. Audio

`engine_render` (the audio block) calls `live_bridge_render(n)` **instead of** `seq_advance`
while `live_bridge_active()`. That runs `clip_engine_render` once per block at
`CLIP_ENGINE_FS = 44100` (the sample-counter rate; set in the build scripts) and maps each event:

| clip event | engine command |
|---|---|
| `CLIPEV_DRUM` | `engine_drum(0, lane, accent ? 1.0 : accent_q7/127)` — kit 0 is the 909 |
| `CLIPEV_NOTE_ON` (BASS) | `engine_bass_on(0, note, vel>=100, 0)` — 303 A |
| `CLIPEV_NOTE_ON` (LEAD) | `engine_bass_on(1, note, vel>=100, 0)` — 303 B |
| `CLIPEV_NOTE_OFF` | `engine_bass_off(0/1)` |
| `CLIPEV_MOTION` | `engine_set(b>>4, b&15, a, c)` |

CHORDS/FX map to nothing yet (they have no clips until T5i). A note's `len` already becomes the
gate-off in the clip engine, so a 303 note releases after `len` steps. The commands go through
X0X's command queue, so they appear in the runner's `events` log (the tests assert this).

`live_bridge_exit` stops the clip transport and queues `engine_bass_off` for both 303s before
handing the audio back, so **no note hangs** when LIVE is left (checked below).

### 3. Modes

A long press (>= 600 ms) on **PLAY** enters and leaves LIVE from anywhere; a short press is
play/stop exactly as before. The transport acts on the **press** (unchanged timing — a release
action shifted every existing scenario by the tap's 40 ms), and the long press is detected while
the button is held, showing a filling ring over the current screen. Only PLAY changes mode; HOME
never exits LIVE, and SCL (`B_SEL`) toggles the layer.

### 4. The PADS layer (default)

- The 27 keys are clips in zone order (DRUMS F3–B3, BASS C4–E4, LEAD F4–B4, CHORDS C5–E5,
  FX F5–G5); `song.key_clip` is the identity map.
- Tap a key: launch its clip at the clip's quantize. Tap the playing clip: queue a stop.
  HOME + key: `clip_engine_launch_now`.
- **Key LEDs** (the single-colour levels X0X offers — lit and dim, with blink done by toggling
  each frame, as X0X's own LED pass does):
  **off** = empty slot, **dim** = loaded/stopped, **lit** = playing,
  **fast blink** = queued to start (`ui.frame & 4`), **slow blink** = queued to stop
  (`ui.frame & 32`). The SCL button is lit on the PLAY layer.
- The screen: the header shows the `LIVE` chip, the layer (`PADS`), the transport icon, the BPM
  and `bar.beat`; the body is a 5-row pad grid (role colour, cell state, the playing clip's
  progress bar, the focused row arrowed); the footer reads `SCL play` / `hold PLAY exit`.
  Drawn with `design/project/Live-Pads-*.dc.html`, `Header.dc.html` and `PadGrid.dc.html` and the
  `ui_tokens.h` colours, in `AF_S/AF_M`, at 1:1.

### 5. The PLAY layer

SCL toggles it. The keys play the focused role chromatically (`41 + key + 12*octave`), through
the same engine path (`live_bridge_play_key`). The focused role is the last one launched, or BASS
at entry; ALGORITHM steps it. OCT−/OCT+ shift the octave (−3..+4). The header says `PLAY`; the
screen shows the role in its colour, its engine, the octave, and the 5-cell clip-status strip
(`Live-Play.dc.html`). SCL toggles back.

### 6. Inert controls

- **SEL** is the layer toggle in LIVE (it is the physical SCL button); it confirms nothing else.
- **Turning SELECT does nothing in LIVE** (its detents are read and discarded).
- **K1–K4 are inert in LIVE.** X0X has no global performance knobs to keep — its K1–K4 are the
  current page's values, and LIVE has no page yet. The performance knobs (FILTER/SPACE/ECHO/
  DRIVE) arrive with the punch FX in T10i; the knob strip shows empty cards until then.
- ALGORITHM steps the focused role; PRESETS, EDIT, GLO, SAVE, ARP, SEQ, REC, HOME are inert in
  LIVE (HOME a tap does nothing; HOME + key means launch-now).

## RAM

- `engine.c`: the delay line `dly_buf` is cut from 88,200 to **44,100** samples (1.0 s), and
  `fx_retime` in `fxbus.c` now clamps `dl_time_ms` to `(dlen - 256)` samples after the halving,
  so no delay-time path can overrun the buffer.
- The LIVE `song_t` (14,352 B) and `clip_engine_t` (440 B) go in the `.pool`.

Measured with `make fw` on deltron:

| | before (T2b) | after (T3) |
|---|---|---|
| POOL used | 328,052 / 344,064 | **254,636 / 344,064** |
| `.data+.bss` | 95,044 / 98,304 | **95,124 / 98,304** |
| image | — | 472,704 B |

The delay cut frees ~86 KiB; the song and engine take ~15 KiB of it back, leaving ~89 KiB of
pool against the 8 KiB spare. The +80 B of `.data+.bss` is the LIVE UI state.

### New/changed runner commands (`host/x0x_host.c`)

`expect mode`, `layer`, `focus`, `liveactive`, `liveclipR`, `livequeR`, `livestopR`,
`liveusedC`; `keyblink K [slow]` (a key's LED reaches both a lit and a not-lit level — how the
blink states are checked); `bar`/`beat`/`step`/`playing`/`wait-bar` and the `dump` JSON read the
clip engine while LIVE owns the transport, and `dump` gains a `live` object (active, layer,
focus, transport, per-role clip/queued/stop/progress) and `key_clip`.

## Decisions / open

- **Motion param encoding** is pinned by T3 (`target << 12 | voice << 8 | index`) and noted in
  `model.md`, since the model left the field free.
- **K1–K4 in LIVE** are inert; the design's performance knobs are T10i's.
- The clip engine uses the model `song_t` while X0X has its own `song_t`; the bridge reconciles
  the one shared name by renaming the model's type in its own translation unit, and exposes only
  plain types in `live_bridge.h` so `ui.c` (which has X0X's `song_t` in scope) can include it.
- The key→clip map is built as the identity each entry; LIVE SETUP (T10i) will edit it.
