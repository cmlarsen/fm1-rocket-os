## Build
1. **Audio:** `perform_process` runs on the master stereo signal after the mix and before X0X's output stage, on core 0, in every view while the clip engine plays.
   - It gets the next 1/8 and 1/16 grid positions from the clip engine's transport.
   - The echo-throw controller's targets go to fxbus through the direct ISR-safe path. Engage and release come from the main loop through `perform`'s ring.
   - `perform_t` goes in the pool.
2. **The FX layer in LIVE:** while FX is held:
   - the first 6 lower keys trigger repeat 1/8, repeat 1/16, tape stop, filter dive, echo throw and freeze, where a tap latches and holding is momentary;
   - the upper keys toggle the role mutes;
   - the screen shows the LIVE 6 design (the six FX cells with their active and latched states, the knob strip);
   - the key LEDs show which effects are active.
   Releasing FX returns to the previous layer, and latched effects stay on. HOME+FX resets every effect.
3. **Mutes** go through the clip engine (a role muted mid-note releases its notes).
4. **Leaving LIVE** releases every effect and clears every mute.
5. **CPU:** rc_debug stress 3 runs stress 2 plus all six effects cycling. The coordinator measures it on the device. Report the host profile for `perform`.
