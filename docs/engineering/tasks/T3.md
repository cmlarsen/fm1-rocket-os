## What to build
1. **The song in RAM, `live_bridge.c`.** A `song_t` built from the current X0X project at entry to LIVE:
   - the 909 patterns become DRUMS clips A–G (the first 7 non-empty patterns, `dpart` mapped onto `drum_steps_t`);
   - 303 A patterns become BASS clips, as notes;
   - 303 B patterns become LEAD clips;
   - CHORDS and FX are empty for now.
   Storage is T6i's job, so the song is rebuilt each time LIVE opens. Write this mapping as one plain function with a unit test.
2. **Audio.**
   - `clip_engine_render` runs once per audio block, while the transport plays in LIVE, in place of X0X's sequencer.
   - The sample-counter rate is `FS`.
   - Clip events map to engine commands: DRUMS go to the 909 through `engine_drum` (lane → voice, accent → velocity); BASS goes to 303 A and LEAD to 303 B, through `engine_bass_on/off`, where note length means a gate off after `len` steps; motion events go to `engine_set`.
   - CHORDS and FX produce events but no sound until T5i.
   - Leaving LIVE stops the clip engine and hands control back to X0X's sequencer cleanly, with no hung notes.
3. **Modes.** Long-press PLAY (the hold time is in the interaction model, or else 600 ms) enters LIVE from anywhere and leaves it. A short press on PLAY stays play/stop. Nothing else enters or leaves LIVE.
4. **The LIVE PADS layer.** It is the default on entry.
   - **Key map:** the 27 keys are clips in zone order: DRUMS F3–B3, BASS C4–E4, LEAD F4–B4, CHORDS C5–E5, FX F5–G5. This is `song.key_clip`, and the default is identity.
   - **Key presses:** tapping a key launches its clip with the clip's quantize; tapping the playing clip queues a stop. HOME + key launches now.
   - **Key LEDs** use the single-colour LED levels X0X offers: off for an empty slot, dim for loaded, on for playing, blinking for queued, and a slow blink for "stop queued". Use X0X's LED animation facilities, and say which levels you used.
   - **Screen:** the header shows the mode, the layer, the transport state, BPM and bar.beat. Below it is a pad grid of the 5 zones (role colour from `ui_tokens.h`, cell state, the playing clip's progress) and a footer hint ("SCL play · hold PLAY exit"). Follow `design/project/Live-Pads-*.dc.html` and `Main.dc.html` with the tokens, at 1:1. The fonts are `AF_S/AF_M/AF_L`.
5. **The PLAY layer.** SCL toggles it.
   - The keys play the focused role chromatically, through the same engine path. The focused role is the last role launched, or BASS by default.
   - OCT−/OCT+ shift the octave.
   - The header shows PLAY. The screen shows the role and the octave, following `Live-Play.dc.html`.
   - SCL toggles back to the pads.
6. **Inert controls.** SEL and turning SELECT do nothing in LIVE. K1–K4 keep X0X's performance-knob behaviour, if any; list what they do in the report.

## Tests (write first)
